Single-Portion Serving System

One standard pre-portioned meal for each student
Screen 1 of 5 · Overview

Welcome to the meal service

This concept prepares individual meal containers before peak serving time. Each student initially collects one standard portion. An additional serving may be requested if food is available and mess policy permits.
